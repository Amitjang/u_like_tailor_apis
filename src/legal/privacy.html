Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Englishहिंदी
In short: U Like Tailor is a tool for tailor shops. The shop owner decides what customer data to enter, and that data belongs to the shop. We use data only to run the app, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

1. Who we are

“U Like Tailor” (the “App”) is provided by ishvanetech, Ambala, Haryana, India (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what data the App collects, why, who it is shared with, and your choices. It applies to the U Like Tailor mobile apps (Android and iPhone) and the services and web pages connected to them.

Contact: ishvanetech@gmail.com · Phone / WhatsApp: +91 74968 86049

2. Our role and the shop’s role

The App is used by tailor shop owners and their staff (“Shop Users”) to manage their own business. Two kinds of data are involved:

  • Account data of Shop Users (for example, the owner’s login details). For this data, we decide how it is used and we are responsible for it (a “Data Fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).
  • Shop data that a Shop User enters about their own customers, family members, staff and orders (for example names, phone numbers, measurements and photos). The shop decides what to collect and why; it is the Data Fiduciary for that data. We only store and process it on the shop’s behalf to provide the App (as a “Data Processor”). The shop is responsible for having a lawful reason and, where needed, consent to enter and use its customers’ and staff’s data, including for sending them WhatsApp messages.

If you are a customer of a tailor shop and want your data changed or removed, please contact that shop first. You may also write to us and we will pass your request to the shop and help as far as we can.

4. Phone permissions

  • Camera / Photos: only when you choose to take or pick a photo (customer, design, fabric, staff, shop logo) or scan a garment QR tag. Photos are made smaller on your phone before upload.
  • Microphone / Speech recognition: only when you tap the voice button to fill measurements. Your phone’s speech service (Google or Apple) converts speech to text; the App uses the text. We do not keep audio recordings.
  • Notifications: to remind you about deliveries, trials, dues and plan expiry. You can turn them off in phone settings.

5. How we use data

  • To provide, maintain and improve the App and its features;
  • To send in-app and push notifications you have asked for (for example delivery reminders);
  • To help the shop send messages to its customers (for example “order ready”), at the shop’s request;
  • To process plan purchases and provide receipts;
  • For security, fraud and abuse prevention, and to comply with law.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use shop or customer data for advertising.

6. Service providers we use

We share data only with providers who help us run the App, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting
  • Render — server hosting
  • Cloudinary — photo storage and delivery
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple — login
  • Razorpay (and, where used, Google Play / Apple App Store billing) — plan payments
  • WhatsApp / Meta — when a shop sends a message to its customer through WhatsApp

These providers have their own privacy policies. Some of them may store or process data on servers outside India. We may also disclose data if required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

7. How we protect data

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Staff passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Each shop’s data is kept separate, and the shop owner controls what each staff login can see. Access is limited to what is needed to run the service. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; if we learn of a data breach that affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

8. How long we keep data

We keep data while the account is active. When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymise its data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example payment and tax records), which are kept only for the period the law requires. Copies in backups are removed in the normal backup cycle, usually within 90 days. Shop owners can also delete individual customers, photos and old order photos from inside the App.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you can: access the data we hold about you; ask us to correct or update it; ask us to delete your account and data (see Delete account); withdraw consent (this does not affect earlier processing); nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity; and raise a complaint with us. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

The App is meant for business use by persons aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. Shops should enter a child’s details (for example a child’s measurements) only with the consent of the parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for important changes, tell you in the App. Using the App after the change means you accept the updated policy.
